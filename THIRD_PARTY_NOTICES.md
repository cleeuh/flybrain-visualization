# Third-party notices

## flybody — fruit fly body model

The whole-fly body shown on the tour's opening slides (`data/meshes/fly_*.bmesh`, generated by
`tools/fetch_fly_body.py`) is converted from the **flybody** model:

- Repository: https://github.com/TuragaLab/flybody (commit `d015e9bfe441bd90ae431bac24c55cb74bdbce26`)
- Paper: Vaxenburg R., Siwanowicz I., Merel J., et al. *Whole-body physical simulation of fruit
  fly locomotion.* Nature (2025). bioRxiv 2024, doi:10.1101/2024.03.11.584515
- Licence: Apache License 2.0 — full text in [`licenses/flybody-LICENSE.txt`](licenses/flybody-LICENSE.txt)

Changes made: the model's visual meshes are posed from `fruitfly.xml`, merged by material
(body, eyes, wing membrane, wing veins), converted from centimetres to micrometres, re-oriented
and positioned so the connectome's brain sits inside the head, and written in this project's
`.bmesh` format.

- Repository: https://github.com/NeLy-EPFL/flygym
